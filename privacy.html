Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 19, 2026

Overview

Kore Legion Shield ("Shield," "we," "us") is a legal-protection app. Customers use Shield during police encounters — traffic stops, arrests, and other legal situations — for real-time rights guidance, evidence recording, and automatic notification of their attorney and emergency contacts. This policy explains how we handle information related to SMS messaging as part of that emergency-contact feature.

Who We Message

We only send SMS messages to phone numbers that a Shield customer has listed as their own emergency contact, and only after that specific contact has confirmed they want to receive messages (see "How Consent Works" below). We do not purchase contact lists, send unsolicited messages, or engage in marketing campaigns.

How Consent Works

When a Shield customer adds or changes an emergency contact's phone number, we send that contact exactly one text message asking them to reply YES to confirm they want to receive safety alerts, or STOP to decline. We do not send that contact anything further unless they reply YES themselves. A Shield customer adding someone's number does not, by itself, opt that person in — consent always comes from the contact directly.

What We Send

Once a contact has confirmed, they may receive occasional safety alerts from Shield on behalf of the customer who listed them — for example, if that customer triggers Shield's Emergency Mode or escalates a traffic stop. These messages are infrequent and tied to real events, not scheduled or routine.

Data We Collect

We collect the phone numbers Shield customers provide for their own emergency contacts, and each contact's confirmation status (pending, confirmed, or declined). We do not collect or store personal data from contacts beyond what's needed to deliver alerts and honor their opt-out.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share phone numbers or message content with third parties for marketing purposes. Numbers are shared only with the messaging carrier required to deliver the SMS.

Opt-Out

Any contact can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. After opting out, no further messages will be sent to that number, regardless of what any Shield customer has on file.

Help

Reply HELP to any message for assistance, or contact us at edward@korelegion.com.

Contact

For questions about this policy or your data, contact us at edward@korelegion.com.
