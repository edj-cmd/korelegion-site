Emergency Contact SMS Opt-In

How Kore Legion Shield gets consent before texting anyone.

How it works

  1. A Shield customer lists you as their emergency contact in the app, entering your name and phone number.
  2. You receive one text message from Kore Legion Shield asking you to confirm.
  3. You reply YES to start receiving alerts, or STOP to decline. If you don't reply, you receive nothing further.
  4. Only after you reply YES will you receive occasional safety alerts — for example, if that customer triggers an emergency or an escalated traffic stop.

Being listed as someone's emergency contact does not, by itself, sign you up for anything. Consent always comes from you, directly, by replying to our text — never from the person who added you.

Sample opt-in message

From: Kore Legion Shield
"Kore Legion Shield: [Name] added you as an emergency contact on their account. Reply YES to receive occasional safety alerts on their behalf, or STOP to opt out. Msg&data rates may apply."

Opt-out

Reply STOP to any message at any time to opt out — no further messages will be sent to your number, regardless of what any Shield customer has on file. Reply HELP for assistance.

Message frequency & rates

Message frequency varies and is tied to real events, not a fixed schedule. Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier plan.
