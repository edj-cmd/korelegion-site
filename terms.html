Program Description

Kore Legion Shield ("Shield") is a legal-protection app for police encounters. As part of its emergency-contact feature, a Shield customer may list someone as an emergency contact to be notified if the customer triggers an emergency alert or escalates a traffic stop. Shield only sends SMS messages to a listed contact after that specific contact has confirmed, in their own reply, that they want to receive them.

How Opt-In Works

When a Shield customer adds or changes an emergency contact's phone number, that contact receives exactly one text asking them to reply YES to confirm or STOP to decline. Being listed as someone's emergency contact does not by itself enroll you in messaging — you must reply YES yourself before you receive anything further. Consent is not a condition of the Shield customer's use of the service.

Message Frequency

Once confirmed, message frequency varies and is tied to real events — an emergency alert or an escalated traffic stop — not a fixed recurring schedule.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier plan. Kore Legion does not charge recipients for messages received.

Opt-Out Instructions

You can stop receiving messages at any time. Reply STOP to any message to opt out. You will receive a one-time confirmation and no further messages will be sent to your number, regardless of what any Shield customer has on file.
